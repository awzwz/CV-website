## 1. PM / APM / TPM internships at large tech companies: what is open, which accept MS (non-MBA) students, which accept internationals

### Takeaway
By 27 Oct 2026 most flagship big-tech PM intern postings for Summer 2027 were already closed: Google US APM and PM, Microsoft PM (Redmond and Mountain View), Salesforce APM, Databricks, Atlassian, Visa APM, Duolingo and Datadog. The open big-name PM roles are Amazon (MBA-only ALA PM intern, plus a PMT intern that requires an advanced degree), Intuit (requires US work authorization, no sponsorship), Visa (PM Intern, Data & AI), Coinbase APM, Roblox, Workday, TikTok/ByteDance, American Express and some MS/MBA roles (GM). No Summer 2027 PM-intern posting was tracked for Apple, Uber, LinkedIn, Stripe, Figma, Notion, Airbnb, DoorDash or Adobe. A non-MBA master's student does qualify for Google APM (BS/MS), Microsoft PM and most "Product Management Intern" roles. The real blocker is US work authorization or J-1 sponsorship, not the degree.

### Inferences
- Multinational graduate/rotational programs (UFLP and similar) are usually country-hired and posted roughly Oct–Jan for the following year. ## 4. Structured early-talent programs that exclude master's students (STEP, Explore, etc.)

### Takeaway
Google STEP and Microsoft Explore are **not available to master's students**. Both are for first- or second-year bachelor's students, and STEP also requires a North American university. Of the PM programs, Google's US APM internship ("penultimate year"), Google UK APM (penultimate or final year, BA or MA), Microsoft PM intern (master's allowed with at least one semester remaining) and Intuit PM intern (any degree program, graduating after the internship) are master's-compatible. The Amazon ALA PM intern and the NVIDIA, Capital One and other MBA PM interns require an MBA.

## 5. Visa / J-1 feasibility for a Kazakhstan-enrolled student, and timeline realism (Nov 2026 – Feb 2027)

### Takeaway
The J-1 Intern/Student-Intern category is designed for exactly this profile: a student currently enrolled at a non-US post-secondary institution who returns to finish the degree. It allows up to 12 months per degree level. However, the host company has to be willing to arrange J-1 sponsorship. Several 2027 postings (Google US PM intern: "must be located in the US"; Intuit: US work authorization, no H-1B sponsorship) effectively assume US-based students on F-1/CPT. Evidence that Google and Meta bring non-US-school interns on J-1 is anecdotal (Blind, 2019–2022) and partly conflicting. On timing, the big-tech PM window for Summer 2027 ran Aug–Oct 2026 and has largely closed. Nov 2026–Feb 2027 is still realistic for late or second-wave postings (Amazon, Visa, AmEx, TikTok, mid-size firms), for Almaty consulting (BCG's deadline was late January in 2025), for multinational graduate programs and for local Kazakhstan programs. It is not realistic for Google, Microsoft, Salesforce or Databricks PM.

### Cited Findings
**J-1 rules**
- J-1 student intern: the applicant must be currently enrolled in and pursuing a degree at an accredited post-secondary institution outside the US. The internship must advance the home degree, and the student must return to complete it. Maximum 12 months per degree or major, with no extensions. Also requires English proficiency, funding and State Department-compliant health insurance. — [Dartmouth OVIS](https://ovis-intl.dartmouth.edu/immigration/j-1-exchange-visitors/categories/student-interns/eligibility); [UT Austin ISSS](https://global.utexas.edu/isss/department-resources/hiring/j1-exchange-visitor-interns)
- University-hosted J-1 student-intern policies **conflict on degree level**. Some frame it as undergraduate-only (e.g. UC Denver's page title "Undergraduate Student Enrolled Abroad"). Others allow Bachelor's or Master's students and exclude doctoral students. — [UC Denver](https://www.ucdenver.edu/services/international-student-and-scholar-services/hr-partners/j-1-scholar/invite-a-j-1-student-intern-(undergraduate-student-enrolled-abroad)); [Arizona](https://international.arizona.edu/international-students/j-1-student-intern)
- The 2008 State Department rule created the student-intern subcategory for foreign students "enrolled and pursuing a degree at post-secondary academic institutions outside the United States", with up to 12 months per degree level. The current eCFR text of 22 CFR 62.22 ("Trainees and interns") was last amended 10 Aug 2026; the content of that amendment was not verified. — [Federal Register 2008 (E8-13799)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2008-06-20/pdf/E8-13799.pdf); [eCFR 62.22](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/chapter-I/subchapter-G/part-62/subpart-B/section-62.22); [Cornell LII 62.22](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/22/62.22)

**Company signals (mostly anecdotal)**
- A Blind thread says CPT applies only to F-1 students at US schools, so a student abroad needs a J-1. A commenter claims Google sponsors J-1 visas for foreign interns who are not at US schools. — [Blind thread](https://www.teamblind.com/post/do-google-andor-other-companies-recruit-international-intern-o3kyjggi)
- On Meta, a 2022 Blind thread says J-1 is the visa Meta uses for such interns. A 2019 post claimed Facebook had stopped hiring non-North-American J-1 interns. These conflict. — [Blind Meta thread](https://www.teamblind.com/post/internship-at-meta-as-a-canadian-citizen-gjuvgkgh); [Blind J1 search](https://www.teamblind.com/search/J1)
- Microsoft: a 2019 UCSB page says product-team interns are placed in Redmond or Sunnyvale and that Microsoft "can also help with visa applications". This is dated. — [UCSB GradPost 2019](https://gradpost.ucsb.edu/blog/money/2019/01/22/microsoft-intern-researcher-opportunities-for-phd-students)

### Inferences
- Under the private-sector J-1 "Intern" category, a US company works with a State-Department-designated sponsor (e.g. Cultural Vistas, CIEE or Intrax, which were not verified in this research). The definition in 22 CFR 62.22, based on prior knowledge and **not re-verified here**, also covers people who graduated from a foreign institution within 12 months. Check the [Cornell LII text](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/22/62.22). If correct, a master's student is eligible under that category even where university-hosted "student intern" policies are undergraduate-only. PhD students may be routed to other J-1 categories (research scholar, or student intern depending on the sponsor).
