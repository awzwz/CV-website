Method note: The research ran on October 27, 2026. Direct page fetching was blocked for most career domains (google.com, metacareers.com, amazon.jobs, greenhouse, workday and others). Only microsoft.com pages could be fetched directly. Most facts below therefore come from search-engine extracts of official postings, or from daily-updated GitHub job trackers that link to official postings: [speedyapply 2027-AI-College-Jobs](https://github.com/speedyapply/2027-AI-College-Jobs) and [SimplifyJobs Summer2027-Internships](https://github.com/SimplifyJobs/Summer2027-Internships). The shared web-search budget ran out before J-1 questions for Ai2, TRI and IBM could be checked; those are listed as gaps.

## Q1. Which programs accept students from universities outside the US, and which sponsor a J-1? Program-by-program list

### Takeaway
- **Meta (PhD only)** explicitly says it accepts PhD students "from all over the world". No official confirmation of J-1 sponsorship was found.
- **Microsoft Research** says it "can help with visa applications where needed". It has dozens of Summer 2027 research-intern postings open right now (Redmond, New England/Cambridge MA, NYC). Most are PhD-only.
- **Google's US Student Researcher and Research Intern postings** list "currently attending a degree program in the United States" as a *preferred* qualification. They require the intern to be physically in the US. Google's EMEA versions require EMEA enrollment, and at least one says there is no immigration sponsorship. A Kazakhstan-enrolled student is therefore at a structural disadvantage at Google.
- For most other labs (NVIDIA, Amazon, Apple, Adobe, Salesforce, Ai2, TRI, IBM, Bosch, Snap, Roblox), no source states their J-1 policy for students enrolled abroad. IBM, Bosch and Intel postings state "no sponsorship" for at least some roles.

### Cited Findings

**General J-1 framework (applies to all industry hosts)**
- J-1 Intern category: the intern must be a foreign national "currently enrolled in and pursuing studies at a foreign degree- or certificate-granting post-secondary academic institution outside the United States", or have graduated from one within the past 12 months. — [BridgeUSA / State Dept, Intern program](https://j1visa.state.gov/programs/intern/)
- J-1 intern stays can last up to 12 months, and the internship must relate to the field of study. The first step is to find a State-Department-designated sponsor. — [Cultural Vistas, J-1 Intern/Trainee](https://culturalvistas.org/our-programs/j1services/j1-visa-sponsorship-intern-trainee)
- Tech companies typically use third-party designated sponsors. Cultural Vistas says it has sponsored visitors at many Fortune 100/500 companies but names none. Intrax shows logos such as Figma, SpaceX and Jane Street. A Waterloo student guide advises asking your recruiter whether the company uses Cultural Vistas as the visa sponsor. — [Cultural Vistas tech sector](https://culturalvistas.org/impact-learning/news-stories/why-cultural-vistas-is-the-top-choice-for-j-1-sponsorship-in-the-tech-sector); [Intrax host companies](https://www.globalinternships.com/partners/companies); [Unofficial Waterloo USA Intern Guide](https://stephenholiday.com/Unofficial-Waterloo-USA-Intern-Guide/)
- PhD students may need a different J-1 category from Student Intern:
  - Texas A&M's ISSS says it sponsors Student Interns only for Bachelor's or Master's students and will not sponsor PhD students in that category. — [Texas A&M ISSS](https://global.tamu.edu/isss/scholars-interns/applying-for-status.html)
  - Universities also use a "J-1 Visiting Research Student" category for students enrolled abroad who do degree-related research. — [Rutgers visa types](https://global.rutgers.edu/visa-types)
  - The J-1 Research Scholar category requires an invitation from a qualifying US host. — [American Immigration Council](https://www.americanimmigrationcouncil.org/practice-advisory/j1-research-scholar/)
- A prior J-1 student stay can trigger a 12-month bar before a later J-1 Research Scholar stay. — [Univ. of Cincinnati, J-1 Scholar](https://www.uc.edu/about/international/visa/faculty/scholar.html)

## Q3. US national-lab, university and institute summer research programs: which are open to international graduate students, and which are US-citizen-only?

### Takeaway
- **US-citizen/permanent-resident only:** DOE SULI and DOE SCGSR. NSF REU funding is also citizen/PR-only.
- **Undergraduate-only, and/or limited to students already enrolled at US institutions:** MIT MSRP, CMU RISS and Caltech SURF. These are not usable by a graduate student enrolled in Kazakhstan.
- **Most realistic academic routes for an international graduate student:**
  - A faculty-sponsored **Visiting Student Researcher** appointment on a J-1 (for example Stanford VSR or Berkeley VSR). These require cold-emailing a professor, and the student usually pays or self-funds.
  - Flatiron Institute's summer/predoctoral positions (international eligibility unconfirmed).
  - The SFI Complex Systems Summer School, which is open worldwide but is a school, not an internship.

### Cited Findings
- **DOE SULI:** applicants "must be United States Citizen or Lawful Permanent Resident at the time of application". — [DOE SULI Eligibility](https://science.osti.gov/wdts/suli/Eligibility)
- **DOE SCGSR:** US citizen or LPR, and "an F1-visa student is not eligible". Requires full-time enrollment in a US graduate program. — [DOE SCGSR FAQ](https://science.osti.gov/wdts/scgsr/Frequently-Asked-Questions)
- **NSF REU** scholarships (as used by CMU RISS) require US citizenship, national status or permanent residency. — [CMU RISS scholarships](https://riss.ri.cmu.edu/scholarships)
- **CMU RISS:**
  - Only "for current undergraduate students" with at least one semester remaining.
  - Most non-US participants come on a J-1 intern visa, and funding is available for international students.
  - The 2026 cycle applications ran Nov 1 to Jan 21; the program ran Jun 1 to Aug 9.
  - Source: [CMU RISS FAQ](https://riss.ri.cmu.edu/faq/); [RISS international/immigration FAQ](https://riss.ri.cmu.edu/faq_category/international-scholars-immigration-questions/)
- **MIT MSRP:** applications only from students "currently enrolled at a home institution located in the United States or its territories". Students at institutions outside the US are not eligible. Undergraduates only (sophomore to non-graduating senior). — [MIT OGE MSRP FAQ](https://oge.mit.edu/msrp/frequently-asked-questions/)
- **Caltech SURF:** undergraduates only. International visiting students may do SURF on the Caltech campus, but JPL placements are US citizens/PR only. — [Caltech SFP SURF eligibility](https://sfp.caltech.edu/undergraduate-research/programs/surf/eligibility_requirements)

  - 2027 postings were not yet found. — [Simons Foundation internship opportunities](https://www.simonsfoundation.org/careers/internship-opportunities/)

  - For a student enrolled in a graduate program at another university who does research under a Stanford faculty member. Any Stanford faculty member may sponsor.
  - J-1 is the permissible visa, with pay via hourly employment or a fellowship.
  - Example 2025–26 costs at the Graduate School of Education: $1,377/month registration fee plus proof of $4,815/month living expenses.

### Inferences
- **A practical playbook for a Kazakhstan-based candidate:**
  1. Apply formally to 15–30 specific postings now (late Oct to mid-Dec 2026).
  2. In parallel, email 1–2 named researchers per target team, ideally authors of papers close to your own work. Include a 3–4 line pitch, a link to a paper or preprint or GitHub, and the posting ID you applied to.
  3. Ask recruiters early whether they host interns enrolled at non-US universities and which J-1 sponsor they use.
  4. Keep a backup: a faculty-hosted VSR, or a non-US lab office of the same companies (Amazon Bengaluru, Google or Microsoft EMEA sites, if eligible).
- **For a master's student without publications,** the realistic targets are Ai2, Amazon MS applied science, Adobe, Salesforce and Google BS/MS Student Researcher. Strong arXiv preprints, open-source work or competition results partly substitute for publications.
