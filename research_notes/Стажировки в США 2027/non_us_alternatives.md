## Q1. Europe/UK: which 2027 programs exist, what is their status, and which accept non-EU students (enrolled outside the EU) with visa support?

### Takeaway
Many 2027 EMEA programs are live now. Google's deadlines are about 13–14 Dec 2026, Revolut is recruiting on a rolling basis through Dec 2026, and Wise is closing about now. But most big-tech EMEA student programs either require enrollment at an EMEA university or require existing work rights. Only one large European employer explicitly offers visa, travel and accommodation support to international interns: **Revolut**, at its UK, Poland, Portugal and **UAE (Dubai)** hubs. **JetBrains** (EU, UK, Serbia and Armenia only, no visa support) and **Booking.com** (Dutch university plus EEA work rights) are effectively closed to a student enrolled in Kazakhstan.

  - Employer fees reported: about £600+VAT (Access Tier 5) and £750+VAT (Tier 5 Intern). These figures may be outdated. — [Imperial](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/students/international-students/visas-and-immigration/working-in-the-uk/working-in-the-uk-after-your-studies/government-authorised-exchange/); [SGUL](https://sgul.ac.uk/study/life-at-st-georges/international-student-support/working-in-the-uk/working-in-the-uk-after-your-studies/tier-5-government-authorised-exchange)

- *Germany*:
  - A residence permit for an internship is possible for non-EU students if it is study-related, typically after the 4th semester, or part of an exchange/EU-funded scheme. — [German Embassy Lisbon](https://lissabon.diplo.de/pt-de/service/-/2423740); [German Embassy Bern](https://bern.diplo.de/ch-de/service/visa/national-visa/employment/internship/2640448)

- *Netherlands* (student at a non-Dutch university): the employer applies to UWV for a TWV work permit. The internship must be at HBO/WO level with an internship agreement, and the intern needs sufficient funds (reported as at least 50% of Dutch minimum wage). It takes about 5 weeks, and work cannot start before the permit is issued. — [IamExpat](https://www.iamexpat.nl/career/employment-news/what-are-dutch-internship-immigration-rules-non-eu-nationals)
- *Switzerland*: a non-EU intern's placement must be an "integral part of the enrolled study", and the Swiss employer applies. The maximum is 12 months, allowed once, and the company's short-term foreign staff must not exceed a quarter of its workforce. This is university guidance and may target Swiss-enrolled students. — [ZHAW PDF](https://www.zhaw.ch/storage/hochschule/career-services/upload/Overview_work_permit_regulation_Switzerland.pdf); [Canton Zurich](https://www.zh.ch/en/wirtschaft-arbeit/erwerbstaetigkeit-auslaender/studierende-und-praktikanten.html)

## Q2. Gulf (UAE): which programs are open to Central Asian master's/PhD students?

### Takeaway
There is no confirmed, open 2027 big-tech or AI-lab internship in the Gulf that targets external master's or PhD students. MBZUAI's flagship UGRIP is **undergraduate-only**. Its internship guide is for MBZUAI's *own* students. The most concrete Gulf option is **Revolut's Dubai hub** (Internship Programme 2027, with visa support). Kazakh citizens appear to have visa-free *entry* to the UAE (sources conflict), but an internship still needs an employer-arranged work permit.

## Q5. Kazakhstan-citizen visa specifics (entry vs. right to intern)

### Takeaway
Visa-free *entry* for Kazakh citizens (UAE (likely), Korea, China, Singapore: about 30 days) never authorizes an internship. Every destination needs an employer- or sponsor-driven work or training authorization. The **Schengen area, the UK and Japan require a visa even to enter**. The most tractable routes are those built for students of foreign universities: Japan's Designated Activities internship visa, Singapore's TEP, the UK's GAE via an overarching body, and the German, Dutch and Swiss study-related internship permits. All of them reward having a **university-endorsed, curricular internship agreement** from the Kazakh home university.

### Cited Findings
- **Schengen (EU)**: visas are required. The EU has a mandate to negotiate visa facilitation with Kazakhstan: faster processing, fewer documents, lower fees and more multi-entry visas. In 2023 Kazakh citizens filed about 159,000 Schengen applications, with 143,970 approved and 14,285 rejected. — [Astana Times](https://astanatimes.com/2025/04/kazakhstan-eu-move-closer-to-signing-visa-facilitation-agreement/); [Council of the EU doc](https://data.consilium.europa.eu/doc/document/ST-6681-2025-INIT/en/pdf); [Orda.kz](https://en.orda.kz/eu-begins-preparing-visa-facilitation-agreement-with-kazakhstan-5698)
- **UK**: internships require the GAE route with sponsorship; see Q1. — [GOV.UK (archived)](https://tnaqa.mirrorweb.com/ukgwa/20260505190059mp_/https://www.gov.uk/government-authorised-exchange/print)
- **UAE**: likely visa-free entry for ordinary passports (2017 protocol), but sources conflict. A student training permit requires UAE residency. — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/uae/government/uae-and-kazakhstan-amend-visa-waiver-agreement-1.2032321); [u.ae](https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/jobs/employment-and-training-of-minors/temporary-students-work-permits-and-training-permits)
- **Japan**: a visa is required, with no eVisa. The internship visa needs a home-university agreement and a Certificate of Eligibility. — [PeninsulaVisa](https://www.peninsulavisa.com/visa-requirements/kazakhstan-to-japan); [VisaJapan HR](https://hr.visajapan.jp/qa/q_intern5.html)
- **Korea**: 30 days visa-free with K-ETA for tourism; work and study need a visa. — [Kazinform](https://www.inform.kz/en/kazakhstan-south-korea-resume-visa-free-regime-after-covid-19-uncertainty_a3917509)

Japan needs a Certificate of Eligibility before the visa. — [IamExpat](https://www.iamexpat.nl/career/employment-news/what-are-dutch-internship-immigration-rules-non-eu-nationals); [NUS annex PDF](https://medicine.nus.edu.sg/dbmi/wp-content/uploads/sites/27/2025/07/DBMI-Internship-Annex-A.pdf); [Embassy of Japan in Germany PDF](https://www.de.emb-japan.go.jp/files/100875033.pdf)
