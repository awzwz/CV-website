## Q1. Which large US companies accept students enrolled outside the US for US internships and sponsor a J-1 (vs. requiring F-1 CPT or existing US work authorization)?

### Takeaway
**No large US tech company says outright on a 2027 intern posting "we hire students at non-US universities on J-1."** The evidence is indirect:
- Microsoft's student-careers page states it provides visa sponsorship and hires students from universities worldwide.
- Meta's intern postings only require you to obtain work authorization by the time of hire, rather than to have it already.
- Big-tech J-1 intern hiring of non-US-enrolled students is well documented through Canadian co-op students (Waterloo uses the J-1 sponsors Cultural Vistas and Intrax) and through Facebook's J-1 intern program.

On the other side:
- Google's US SWE intern postings are labelled "United States-only" programs.
- Anthropic's Fellows program explicitly does not sponsor visas.
- Intel states it does not sponsor candidates who have or held a J-1.

The J-1 itself is issued through a State-Department-designated sponsor (for example Cultural Vistas, Intrax or CIEE), not by the employer.

### Cited Findings
**J-1 rules (who qualifies, who sponsors)**
- **Who qualifies.** The J-1 "Intern" category covers people currently enrolled full-time in a degree program at a post-secondary institution outside the US, or who graduated no more than 12 months before the start date. It lasts up to 12 months and the internship must be in the field of study. — [Cultural Vistas](https://culturalvistas.org/our-programs/j1services/j1-visa-sponsorship-intern-trainee); [Georgia Tech ISSS](https://isss.oie.gatech.edu/node/3317); [UC Berkeley Intl Office](https://internationaloffice.berkeley.edu/ucb_departments/J-1/student-interns)
- **You must return to finish the degree.** The internship must fulfil the educational objectives of the home-institution degree, and the student must return home to complete it. This fits a Kazakhstan master's student doing a summer internship. — [Georgia Tech ISSS](https://isss.oie.gatech.edu/j-student-intern-program-georgia-tech)
- **The sponsor is not the company.** It is a State-Department-designated organization (CIEE, Cultural Vistas, AIPT and others) that issues the DS-2019 and reviews the training plan. You cannot apply for a J-1 without one. — [MigrateMate J-1 SWE intern page](https://migratemate.co/j1-jobs/software-engineer-intern) (commercial job board; treat with care); [Waterloo co-op J-1 page](https://uwaterloo.ca/co-operative-education/work-abroad/visa-permits/us-j1-visa)

**Precedent: Waterloo co-op students placed in the US on J-1**
- The University of Waterloo, which sends many co-op interns to US tech companies, lists Cultural Vistas and Intrax as its preferred J-1 sponsors.
- Its employer guide tells US employers to use one of them.
- Some employers handle J-1 sponsorship through their own immigration lawyers instead.
- Who pays the fees is decided case by case.
- Sources: [Waterloo co-op J-1](https://uwaterloo.ca/co-operative-education/work-abroad/visa-permits/us-j1-visa); [Waterloo "Process for U.S. J-1 visa" (employer guide)](https://uwaterloo.ca/hire/employer-resources/process-us-j-1-visa)
- A 2021 personal "US intern guide" from a Canadian student describes going through Intrax. It notes that the process differs "if you're on Cultural Vistas or company-specific sponsors", which suggests some big-tech hosts use their own designated sponsor arrangements. — [irenexychen.com guide (2021)](https://irenexychen.com/blog/2021/08/us-intern-guide-covid)

### Inferences
- **The best-documented big-tech J-1 path is the "Canadian co-op model".** The company extends an offer, then a designated sponsor (Cultural Vistas or Intrax) or the company's own counsel issues the DS-2019. A Kazakhstan-enrolled master's student fits the J-1 Intern category on paper (enrolled abroad, field-related internship, returns to finish the degree).
- **Microsoft is the company with the most explicit official pro-sponsorship and global-hiring language.** Meta's posting wording also leaves room for J-1. Google's US SWE intern postings are explicitly "US-only program", which in practice favors US-enrolled (F-1 CPT) students. Google research tracks such as Student Researcher accept relocation to the US.
- **Avoid these when looking for a J-1:** roles flagged 🇺🇸 (Amazon Leo, ADC/government cloud, Salesforce Security/Government, defense primes) and companies with explicit no-J-1 statements (Intel; Anthropic Fellows).
- **A missing 🛂 flag in a tracker usually means the company supports F-1 students' CPT/OPT.** It does not guarantee the company will set up a J-1 for a non-US-enrolled student. The candidate should ask the recruiter explicitly: "I am enrolled at a university outside the US; would you host me on a J-1 via your designated sponsor?"
- **For research-heavy ML roles,** the PhD- or MS-research-oriented postings (Microsoft Research, Meta Research Scientist Intern, NVIDIA Research, Google Student Researcher) are historically the most international cohorts. This is inference, not verified for 2027.

### Gaps
- No 2027 official FAQ or posting text was retrieved that says "students at non-US universities are eligible and J-1 is sponsored" for Google, Amazon, Apple, NVIDIA, Netflix, Salesforce, Uber, Stripe, Databricks, Snowflake, OpenAI, Palantir, Bloomberg, Cloudflare, Figma, Datadog, Qualcomm, IBM, Oracle or Tesla. The official sites were blocked here and the search budget was exhausted.
- No 2025–2026 Reddit or Blind first-hand reports were retrieved; Reddit and Blind were blocked from fetching.
- Kazakhstan-specific questions are unresearched and need checking:
  - J-1 appointment wait times at US Embassy Astana / Consulate General Almaty.
  - Whether the two-year home-residency rule (INA 212(e), Exchange Visitor Skills List) would apply to a Kazakhstan citizen in CS/IT.
- Intel's no-J-1 statement: its exact wording and scope (intern vs. full-time) were not read in full.

## Q4. Which companies hire international interns at US offices through global programs (Microsoft global hiring, Google international, Amazon international, etc.)?

### Takeaway
Only Microsoft publicly frames its university internships as global hiring with visa sponsorship. Meta (Facebook) has a documented J-1 intern history.

Google and Amazon mostly run country-specific programs:
- Google: US, Canada and EMEA postings; a separate Canada STEP that requires a Canadian university; a US-only MS program.
- Amazon: separate US, UK and Canada SDE intern postings; Europe Applied Scientist postings.

No "international internship" program at Google or Amazon that brings non-US-enrolled students to US offices was found documented.

### Cited Findings
- **Microsoft:** "provides visa sponsorship" and hires students from "thousands of universities across the globe." — [Microsoft University internships](https://careers.microsoft.com/v2/global/en/universityinternship)
- **Google:** internships "across the globe"; visa requirements vary by posting. The US MS SWE intern is a "United States-only program". The Canada STEP requires attending a Canadian university. — [Google Internship FAQs](https://careers.google.com/stories/google-internship-faqs); [Google MS posting](https://www.google.com/about/careers/applications/jobs/results/94172495052972742-software-engineering-intern-ms-summer-2027)
- **Google Student Researcher:** candidates in other countries "must relocate to the United States." This is the one Google track that explicitly contemplates bringing non-US residents to US offices. — [Google Student Researcher posting](https://google.com/about/careers/applications/jobs/results/131518356678156998-student-researcher)
- **Amazon:** separate country postings exist for Summer 2027:
  - SDE Intern for London / Edinburgh / Bristol / Cambridge (UK) and Vancouver (Canada). — [Simplify](https://github.com/SimplifyJobs/Summer2027-Internships)
  - The 2026 Applied Scientist Intern ran European/UK/Israel/UAE/South Africa versions for MS/PhD students. — [Gradcracker](https://www.gradcracker.com/hub/956/amazon/graduate-job/80982/2026-applied-scientist-intern-3-6-months-must-be-studying-towards-a-masters-phd); [JobTeaser 2026 Amazon AS intern](https://www.jobteaser.com/de/job-offers/b687aeb5-9a1e-4c01-a0ca-14a429830ca1-amazon-2026-applied-scientist-intern-amazon-university-talent-acquisition)
- **Meta / Facebook:** J-1 intern program described by the State Department (2015). Meta postings require obtaining work authorization by hire. — [j1visa.state.gov](https://j1visa.state.gov/j-1-participants-gain-cutting-edge-experience-in-silicon-valley-2/); [Meta posting](https://metacareers.com/jobs/1647923135778238)
- **Jane Street:** arranges visas for its Hong Kong internship. No US J-1 evidence was found. — [UNSW careers listing](https://careers.business.unsw.edu.au/graduate-employers/jane-street/jobs-internships/trading-desk-operations-engineer-internship)
- **Negative examples:** Anthropic Fellows (no visa sponsorship) and Intel (no J-1). — [Accel mirror](https://jobs.accel.com/companies/anthropic/jobs/74048366-anthropic-fellows-program-ml-systems-reinforcement-learning); [Intel support](https://intel.com/content/www/us/en/support/articles/000089374/programs/jobs-at-intel.html)
- **Tracker note:** speedyapply maintains a separate "International Positions" list with 791 internships. It lists non-US-located roles, not visa sponsorship to the US. — [speedyapply 2027 SWE](https://github.com/speedyapply/2027-SWE-College-Jobs)

### Inferences
- **For a Kazakhstan student, rank US-office targets roughly as:**
  1. Microsoft (explicit global and sponsorship statement).
  2. Meta and Amazon (language and history compatible with J-1).
  3. NVIDIA, Waymo and other Alphabet units, Netflix, Stripe and Databricks (unknown; ask recruiters).
  4. Google US SWE (US-only program; low odds) and Intel or Anthropic (explicit no).
- **A parallel and often more realistic path:** the same companies' EMEA internships (London, Dublin, Zurich, Warsaw, Munich). These usually handle EU/UK work permits and often run a separate recruiting window. They are not researched here; flag them for another researcher.

### Gaps
- No 2026–27 documentation was found for a named "global intern program" at Amazon, Apple, NVIDIA or Google that relocates non-US-enrolled students to the US.
- Whether Microsoft's sponsorship statement covers J-1 for non-US-enrolled students, as opposed to F-1 CPT for US-enrolled students, is not specified in the snippet.
